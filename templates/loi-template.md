## 1. Parties

**Buyer:** [BUYER]
**Seller:** [SELLER]
**Business:** [BUSINESS] (the "Business")
**Date:** [DATE]

This Letter of Intent ("LOI") sets forth the non-binding understanding between [BUYER] (the "Buyer") and [SELLER] (the "Seller") regarding the proposed acquisition of substantially all the assets of [BUSINESS] (the "Business").

## 2. Non-Binding Statement of Intent

Except for the confidentiality provisions in Section 9 and the exclusivity provisions in Section 7, this Letter of Intent is non-binding and is intended solely to outline the principal terms of a potential transaction. No legally binding obligation will be created unless and until the parties execute a definitive Asset Purchase Agreement and related closing documents (the "Definitive Agreement"). Either party may terminate negotiations at any time prior to execution of the Definitive Agreement.

## 3. Purchase Price and Structure

The total proposed purchase price for the Business is **[PURCHASE PRICE]** (the "Purchase Price"), structured as follows:

- **Cash at Closing:** [CASH AT CLOSE] — payable by wire transfer or certified funds at closing, net of the Earnest Money Deposit and subject to customary prorations and adjustments.
- **Seller Note:** [SELLER NOTE] — promissory note payable to the Seller, bearing interest at [INTEREST RATE]% per annum, amortized over [NOTE TERM] months, with [BALLOON/NO BALLOON] at maturity. The Seller Note shall be subordinate to any senior acquisition financing and placed on standby (interest accruing, payments deferred) for the first [STANDBY MONTHS] months.
- **Working Capital Peg:** The Purchase Price assumes a normalized working capital level at closing of [WORKING CAPITAL PEG]. Any shortfall or surplus relative to the peg shall adjust the Cash at Closing dollar-for-dollar.

The Purchase Price is subject to adjustment based on the final inventory count, FF&E appraisal, and verification of the financial representations during the due diligence period.

## 4. Assets Included

The proposed transaction includes the transfer of substantially all assets used in the operation of the Business, including:

- All tangible personal property, equipment, machinery, vehicles, furniture, fixtures, and tooling (collectively, "FF&E"), free and clear of liens.
- All inventory (parts, supplies, consumables) at cost value as of the closing date.
- All customer lists, contracts, service agreements, and recurring revenue arrangements (the "Book of Business").
- All intellectual property, trade names, trademarks, phone numbers, URLs, websites, and social media accounts associated with the Business.
- All business records, manuals, SOPs, and proprietary processes.
- Any assignable leases for the Business premises, subject to landlord consent and assignability.

## 5. Assets Excluded

The proposed transaction **excludes** the following:

- Cash and cash equivalents on hand as of the closing date.
- Seller's personal vehicles not used in the Business.
- Any liabilities, debts, or obligations of the Seller or the Business incurred prior to the closing date, except as expressly assumed in the Definitive Agreement.
- Any pending or threatened litigation, tax liabilities, or regulatory obligations arising before the closing date.

## 6. Earnest Money Deposit

Upon execution of the Definitive Agreement, the Buyer will deposit **[EMD AMOUNT]** as an earnest money deposit (the "EMD") into an escrow account with a mutually agreed escrow agent. The EMD shall be credited toward the Purchase Price at closing. The EMD shall be refundable to the Buyer until the expiration of the Due Diligence Contingency (Section 8), after which it becomes non-refundable except in the event of Seller default or failure of a financing contingency.

## 7. Exclusivity Period

For a period of **[EXCLUSIVITY DAYS] days** from the date the Seller countersigns this LOI (the "Exclusivity Period"), the Seller agrees not to solicit, negotiate, or accept any other offer or expression of interest regarding the Business from any other party, and agrees not to list the Business for sale with any broker or marketplace. During the Exclusivity Period, the Seller will negotiate exclusively and in good faith with the Buyer toward the Definitive Agreement.

## 8. Due Diligence Contingency

The Buyer's obligation to proceed is contingent on satisfactory completion of due diligence within **[DD DAYS] days** from the date of this LOI (the "Due Diligence Period"). During the Due Diligence Period, the Seller will provide the Buyer and its advisors reasonable access to:

- Three (3) years of federal and state tax returns for the Business.
- Quarterly P&Ls and balance sheets for the most recent twenty-four (24) months.
- Bank statements for the most recent twelve (12) months.
- The lease agreement and any related amendments, plus written confirmation from the landlord of assignability terms.
- Customer lists, contract files, and service agreement documentation.
- Employee roster, payroll records, and any pending HR matters.
- Equipment and vehicle titles, maintenance logs, and any financing statements.

If the Buyer identifies any material adverse fact during due diligence and cannot reach a mutually acceptable resolution with the Seller, the Buyer may terminate this LOI and recover the EMD in full.

## 9. Confidentiality

The terms of this LOI, the existence of these discussions, and any information disclosed by either party in connection with the proposed transaction are confidential. Both parties agree not to disclose such information to any third party (other than their legal, financial, and tax advisors who are bound by professional confidentiality) without the other party's written consent. This confidentiality obligation survives termination of this LOI for a period of two (2) years.

## 10. Closing Date Target

Subject to completion of due diligence, negotiation of the Definitive Agreement, and satisfaction of any financing contingency, the parties target a closing date within **[CLOSE TARGET DAYS] days** from the date of this LOI. The closing shall occur at a mutually agreed time and location, with funds exchanged via wire transfer.

## 11. Governing Law

This LOI shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of **[GOVERNING STATE]**, without regard to its conflict of laws provisions. The confidentiality and exclusivity provisions herein shall be enforceable in any court of competent jurisdiction in that State.

## 12. Signatures

This Letter of Intent reflects the mutual understanding of the parties as of the date written above. Execution below does not create a binding obligation to consummate the transaction, except as expressly stated in Sections 7, 9, and 11.
