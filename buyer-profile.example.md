## Background

I'm a former operations manager with 10 years of experience scaling service businesses. I've managed teams of up to 25 people and overseen P&L for multi-location service operations. I'm transitioning into business ownership through acquisition, targeting Small Main Street businesses that can be acquired with SBA 7(a) financing.

## Investment Thesis

I'm looking for boring, profitable, recession-resistant businesses with predictable cash flow. I prefer service businesses with simple operations, sticky customer bases, and limited dependence on a single key employee. My strategy is to acquire an established business at a fair multiple, maintain stable operations, and grow through operational improvements and modest marketing investment — not through risky transformations.

**Target industries:** Laundromats, car washes, HVAC, plumbing, commercial cleaning, auto repair.

**Financing:** SBA 7(a) with 10% down. I'm pre-qualified with a preferred SBA lender.

## Target Criteria

| Criterion | Target |
|---|---|
| Asking price range | $200,000 – $750,000 |
| SDE range | $75,000 – $300,000 |
| Max SDE multiple | 4.0x |
| Minimum SDE margin | 15% of gross revenue |
| Max employees | 25 |
| Real estate | Not required (lease is fine) |

## Geography

Primary focus: Texas (Austin, San Antonio, Houston metro areas). Open to relocating for the right deal. Max commute: 60 minutes.

## Involvement Level

Full-time owner-operator. I plan to be on-site and hands-on, especially during the first 12 months of transition.

## Deal Structuring Preferences

- SBA 7(a) financing with seller carry-back for 10–20% of the purchase price where possible.
- Prefer deals with at least 3 years of financial history and verifiable tax returns.
- Looking for sellers motivated by retirement with a willingness to provide a 2–4 week transition period.
- Will consider earn-out structures for deals where SDE is near the top of the range.
